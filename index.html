Khafiz Kerimov
I received my PhD in Philosophy from DePaul University in 2020, with a dissertation titled Providence and Prudence in Kant. My research focuses on Kant, Hegel, and German Idealism, with broader interests in 20th century European philosophy, early modern philosophy, American Pragmatism, and the history of ethics.
I am currently an assistant professor (tutor) at St. John’s College, Annapolis, where I teach in the Great Books program across philosophy, the history and philosophy of mathematics and science, literature, and Ancient Greek.
